# Freewheel

A hands-free cockpit for Amp and Codex, running on your Mac.

Say “Hey Puck” or “Hey Codex”, send work, and get a short spoken answer. Freewheel keeps the detailed transcript for when you are back at your screen.

## Development

Implementation is in progress. See [the product direction](docs/product.md) and [research notes](docs/research.md).

Local companion + browser UI. This app intentionally runs on localhost: it needs your installed coding agents and macOS power controls.
